**Domain Scope:** Livestock Health Surveillance & Clinical Field Telemetry (SIH)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation
Livestock farming contributes significantly to rural livelihoods. However, current veterinary care systems face three major bottlenecks:
1. **Fragmented Health History:** Physical paper records lead to lost immunization histories and inaccurate diagnosis.
2. **Delayed Outbreak Detection:** Contagious diseases (e.g., FMD, Lumpy Skin Disease) take days to report to state authorities.
3. **Rural Connectivity Gaps:** Field veterinarians and para-vets operate in remote geographies with intermittent network coverage.

---

## 2. Regulatory & Data Standard Alignment
- **NDLM (National Digital Livestock Mission):** Compliance with 12-digit unique ear-tag identification standards.
- **Bharat Pashudhan Integration:** Interoperability with national animal disease surveillance registries.
- **Data Privacy & Telemedicine Guidelines:** Adherence to veterinary ethical standards for remote consultations and e-prescriptions.

---

## 3. User Personas & Workflow Constraints
- **Dairy Farmer:** Needs intuitive multi-lingual voice/icon navigation, quick appointment scheduling, and automated vaccination reminders.
- **Field Veterinarian:** Requires rapid clinical record entry (<60 seconds), voice notes transcription, offline queueing, and drug dosage calculators.
- **Government Disease Surveillance Officer:** Requires real-time geospatial outbreak density clustering and epidemiological tracking.
