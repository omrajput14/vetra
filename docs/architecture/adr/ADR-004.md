# ADR-004: Requirement of Veterinarian Confirmation for Disease Outbreak Broadcasts

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-07-27

## Context
AI-powered edge models provide rapid preliminary disease predictions (e.g. 94% FMD confidence). However, false positives or unverified public panic can devastate agricultural markets, trigger unnecessary herd culls, and cause economic disruption.

## Decision
We enforce a strict policy: **AI predictions and farmer reports remain private until a licensed Veterinarian clinically audits and confirms the diagnosis**.

## Workflow Enforcement
1. **Farmer Edge Scan**: AI scan produces a preliminary prediction visible *only* to the farmer.
2. **Report Submission**: Farmer chooses to submit a clinical case report to local vets.
3. **Vet Clinical Audit**: A licensed Veterinarian inspects the animal, lab reports, or scan photos.
4. **Rule Engine Validation**: Upon Vet confirmation, the backend PostGIS Rule Engine validates spatial parameters and dispatches radius-based quarantine notifications to surrounding farms.

## Rationale
1. **Prevents Market Panic**: Guarantees that public warnings represent verified clinical outbreaks.
2. **Regulatory & Legal Compliance**: Aligns with government veterinary authority requirements for reportable contagious diseases.
3. **Medical Integrity**: Preserves veterinary authority while using AI for triage.

## Consequences
- Requires active veterinarian review queues for rapid verification turnarounds.
