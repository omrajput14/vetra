VETRA // PITCH CHEAT SHEET PAGE 5 // AI DEFENSE
JUDGE DEFENSE

"If They Attack the AI, Don't Panic"

Six sharp, confident answers to the toughest AI questions panels and judges will throw at you.
Q1. "Does your AI diagnose diseases?"

ANSWER: "No. Vetra provides an AI-assisted preliminary assessment. Definitive clinical diagnosis and prescription treatment remain strictly with a licensed veterinarian."

Q2. "Why not just use ChatGPT?"

ANSWER: "Because a generic chatbot doesn't have the cow's persistent medical context. Vetra's Advisor is connected to the specific animal's identity, species, and historical medical records."

Q3. "What if the AI is wrong?"

ANSWER: "We designed the AI conservatively. It is non-prescriptive, explicitly labels model confidence, provides safety disclaimers, and escalates concerning cases toward veterinary review."

Q4. "Why need this if vets exist?"

ANSWER: "Vetra isn't replacing the veterinarian. It improves what happens before, during, and after the visit by organizing animal history, screening symptoms, and streamlining the care workflow."

Q5. "What is your biggest differentiator?"

ANSWER: "We aren't building just a chatbot, just a booking app, or just an animal ledger. Vetra connects all those pieces around the animal's long-term healthcare journey."

Q6. "What happens after the AI assessment?"

ANSWER: "The farmer moves directly into professional veterinary care, and the doctor's confirmed clinical findings become part of the animal's ongoing immutable medical record."

Presenter Rule: Never argue with a judge. If a judge expresses skepticism about AI, smile and say: "We completely agree with your concern. That is exactly why Vetra treats AI as assistive screening and keeps the licensed veterinarian in total clinical control."
