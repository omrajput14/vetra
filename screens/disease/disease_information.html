Foot and Mouth Disease

Detected on 24 Oct 2023 • Tag ID: A4920

info Overview

Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD) is a severe, highly contagious viral disease. It affects cloven-hoofed animals, including cattle, swine, sheep, and goats. The virus causes illness, severe production losses, and can spread rapidly through contact or contaminated environments.

shield Prevention Tips

Maintain strict biosecurity measures. Ensure all incoming animals are quarantined for 30 days. Restrict farm access to essential personnel only, and require protective clothing and boot washing stations.
